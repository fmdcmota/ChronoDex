# Porto's New Iron Bridge Opens (dom-luis-bridge_1886)

**Status: DRAFT. A human must check every claim against its source before generation spends credits (H7).**

## Summary
The upper deck of the Dom Luís I Bridge, designed by Belgian engineer Théophile Seyrig and built by the Société de Willebroek, was formally inaugurated on 31 October 1886, King Luís I's 45th birthday; the king reportedly stayed away for health reasons. Government, municipal and religious authorities from Porto and Vila Nova de Gaia attended, and Bishop D. Américo blessed the bridge. The lower deck was not yet open, and sources disagree on whether it opened in 1887 or 1888, while the opening toll is given as either 4 or 5 réis per person. Reliable 1886 sources for everyday clothing and food prices were not found, so those details are left out rather than filled in.

## Fun facts
- The ironwork weighed about 3,045 tonnes, and the main arch spans 172 metres.
- The completed structure was load-tested from 26 May 1886 with 2,000 kg per metre.
- A toll of 4 or 5 réis per person, depending on the source, began on 1 November 1886 and lasted until 1 January 1944.
- The bridge carried gas light: 24 lamps on the upper deck, 8 on the lower deck and 8 at the abutments.
- It replaced the Ponte Pênsil suspension bridge of 1843, which had become inadequate for growing traffic.

The upper deck is complete and the arch springs from both granite banks; the lower deck is still unfinished, with unenclosed ironwork below. ## Guide: Joaquim Ferreira, Porter who carries loads across the new upper deck
I am a porter on the Gaia bank, and I earn my bread carrying sacks and crates over the new bridge for anyone who pays the toll. I know every lamp and every rivet of it, because I have walked it a hundred times since the day it opened.

Appearance: A man of about forty, lean and strong-shouldered, with a weathered face and a short beard. He wears a dark flat cap, a coarse wool jacket over a collarless shirt, loose dark trousers and heavy leather boots, with a broad strap over one shoulder for carrying loads.
Voice: eleven-pt-man

### Script
Boa tarde, senhor. I am Joaquim Ferreira, a porter here on the Gaia side, and I will walk a little way with you. They opened this upper deck on the last day of October, the king's birthday, though His Majesty did not come himself. Crossing on foot costs a few réis each, so keep a small coin ready in your pocket. Notice the gas lamps set along the deck; there are twenty-four of them up here, and they burn well after dark. Below us the lower deck is still not finished, so every load goes over the top for now. Mind your step on the iron, and boa viagem.

### Greeting
Boa tarde! You are looking at our new iron bridge, and I am Joaquim, a porter who crosses it every day. Come, I will tell you how it is.
